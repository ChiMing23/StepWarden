# StepWarden · 艾泽拉斯足迹

**一个真的会帮你数步数的插件。**

**An addon that actually counts your steps. Seriously.**

---

## 这是什么 / What Even Is This

你在艾泽拉斯跑了一整天。踩过岩浆，绕过巡逻，为了一个死活不刷新的任务 NPC 来回跑了三趟。

没人记下来。直到现在。

StepWarden 会数你的步数、交通工具次数，以及你"消耗"掉的卡路里，然后在每晚 22:00 把你和朋友们排个高低。

数字是编的。面子是真的。

You ran across Azeroth all day. You stepped in lava, dodged a patrol route, and made three round trips for a quest NPC that refused to spawn.

Nobody wrote any of that down. Until now.

StepWarden counts your steps, your transport rides, and the calories you "burned" — then ranks you against your friends at 22:00 every night.

The numbers are made up. The bragging rights are real.

**步数 / Steps** —— 徒步算，骑地面坐骑跑也算。
Walking counts. Mounted running counts too.

Flight paths, boats, zeppelins, hearthstones and teleports — each counted separately.

Converted per-race. Entertainment only. Do not cite this in a diet plan.

**好友排名 / Friend Ranking** —— 每晚 22:00 结算，结完开始算第二天。
Settled at 22:00 sharp. The next day starts counting right after.

Give a friend a like. Optional whisper included.

A small bar on the edge of your screen showing today's count, live.

Switch in the settings panel. Takes effect instantly, no UI reload.

## 它是怎么算的（以及它哪里在撒谎）/ How It Counts (And Where It Lies)

暴雪从来没给过一个"我走了几步"的 API。所以我们用了最笨的办法：读你的移动速度，对它做积分。

换算校准到徒步约 176 步/分钟。我们查过资料，问过几个真的会跑步的人，然后被他们笑了。

骑坐骑时用的是**每个种族的坐骑步距**。牛头人的科多兽一步跨得最远，所以同一段路，牛头人步数最少。

恭喜，你的种族天赋终于有了实际用途。

Blizzard has never shipped a "how many steps did I take" API. So we did it the dumb way: read your movement speed and integrate it.

The conversion is calibrated to roughly 176 steps per minute on foot. We looked it up, asked people who genuinely run, and got laughed at.

While mounted, it uses a **per-race mount stride**. A Tauren's kodo covers the most ground per step, so a Tauren logs the fewest steps over the same distance.

Congratulations. Your racial finally has a real job.

## 关于排名：为什么榜上常常只有你 / About The Ranking: Why You're Usually Alone On It

排名有个残酷的真相：它只统计**同阵营、同公会、同时在线、而且真的装了本插件**的人。也就是说，大多数晚上你的对手是你自己。

对立阵营的朋友永远上不了榜。不是我们不想，是暴雪的插件消息通道根本没这条线——跨阵营的插件消息会被静默丢弃，连个报错都不给。

所以如果某个好友明明在线、明明装了插件、却始终不上榜，先别骂我们。查三件事：是不是对立阵营、是不是不在同一个公会、是不是根本没装。

Here's the harsh truth: the ranking only counts **same-faction, same-guild, currently-online players who actually installed this addon**. Most nights, your only opponent is yourself.

Friends on the opposite faction will never appear. Not because we don't want them — Blizzard's addon message channel simply doesn't allow it. Cross-faction addon messages get dropped silently, without so much as an error.

So if someone's online, has the addon, and still never shows up: don't blame us. Check three things — opposite faction, different guild, or never installed it.

## 已知的、故意的、以及修不好的限制 / Known, Intentional, and Unfixable Limitations

- **卡路里是编的。** 位面里没有健身房，我们也没有营养师执照。
- **飞行点不计步。** 你坐在鸟背上，扇翅膀的是鸟，不是你。
- **飞行坐骑不计步。** 空中一格抵地上几十格，总得有人划条线。
- **22:00 才结算。** 跟你现实里的健身计划一个毛病——非要等到晚上才想起来。
- **步数不会让你变瘦。** 认真的。

- **The calories are made up.** There are no gyms in the Shadowlands, and we don't have a nutrition license.
- **Flight paths don't count steps.** You're sitting on a bird. The bird is doing the flapping.
- **Flying mounts don't count steps.** One yard in the air is worth dozens on the ground. Someone had to draw a line.
- **Settlement happens at 22:00.** Same problem as your real workout plan — only remembered once evening hits.
- **Steps will not make you thinner.** Genuinely.

## 隐私 / Privacy

数据只写进你自己的 `StepWardenDB`。除了定期广播到公会频道的那点步数，什么都不外传。

我们不要你的账号，不要你的密码，也不要你的灵魂。

Your data lives in your own `StepWardenDB` and nowhere else. Apart from the steps periodically broadcast to your guild channel, nothing leaves your machine.

We don't want your account, your password, or your soul.
